Privacy policy

Agenda plugin for the Desktop Frames + personal build. Last updated September 2026.

In short

The Agenda plugin puts two parties in touch: the computer it runs on, and Google. There is no server in between, nothing is collected, and nothing is shared with anyone else. Whoever wrote this program cannot see anybody's calendar, and has no system in which they could.

What it uses

With the account holder's consent, the plugin reads and writes events in their Google Calendar through the calendar.events scope. Events are drawn in a frame on the desktop and held in memory only while the program is running.

No other scope is requested: not the calendar list, not account settings, not mail, not contacts.

Where things stay

On the account holder's computer. The token Google issues is kept in the program's own folder, encrypted with Windows DPAPI and tied to the Windows account that saved it, so a copy carried anywhere else cannot be read.

What does not happen

  • no calendar data is sent to anyone other than Google
  • no analytics, profiling or usage statistics
  • no data is sold or passed on
  • no account to create: there is nothing to register with

Withdrawing access

At any time, from myaccount.google.com/connections, or by signing out from the plugin's settings. Either way the token held on the computer is deleted.

Contact

Questions and reports: github.com/vittop89/DesktopFramesPlus/issues
